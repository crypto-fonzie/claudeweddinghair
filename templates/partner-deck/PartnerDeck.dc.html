Why we're writing

You don't have a hair problem.
You have a timeline problem.

Every planner and venue coordinator in the GTA has had the same morning. The hair runs long. Photographs get pushed. The ceremony starts late. And the person who has to manage the room, the client and the schedule is you — not the stylist who created it.

We built this business around that one problem.

The morning is the only part of a wedding day that has no slack in it.

Everything after it inherits the delay.

What we promise

Three things, in this order.

Early

Arrival ahead of call time, every wedding. The hair timeline is written and shared with you weeks in advance — not decided in the room.

Yours

The bride still looks like herself. No surprises in the mirror, no renegotiation at 8am while you're waiting.

Holds

Pinned properly, checked before we leave, and a touch-up kit left with the bride for the rest of the day.

Early is the one that matters to you. The other two are why the bride booked us.

Working together

What you get. What we ask.

What you get

  • A named contact — Noelle, directly, not a booking inbox
  • A reply to any enquiry within one working day
  • A written commitment never to overrun the allotted slot

What we ask

  • A recommendation when a bride asks about hair — a name, not a list
  • An introduction to the couple, or a word to the planner beside you
  • The day-sheet six weeks out, so the timeline is real
  • And nothing else. No commission, no exclusivity, no fee

We don't pay for referrals. A recommendation that was bought isn't worth having — yours or ours.
