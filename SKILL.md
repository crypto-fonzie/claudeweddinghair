## Non-negotiables

- Two typefaces only: Instrument Serif (display, Regular + Italic) and Karla (body, 300/400/500). Never a third. Never a script face. Never a synthetic bold.
- Warm neutrals only. No pink, ever. Ink (#241E19) is a warm brown, never #000.
- Corner radius 0 everywhere. No shadows, gradients, glows or blur.
- Left-align body copy. Never justify. Measure 60–70 characters.
- No emoji, no icon libraries. The brass hairpin is the only mark.
- No exclamation marks, no urgency copy, no prices on public-facing pages.
- Banned words: bespoke, luxury, couture, transform, pamper, dream, magical, elevate, journey.
