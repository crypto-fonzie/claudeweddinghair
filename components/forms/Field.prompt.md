Wraps every form control with its label and hint. Labels are uppercase Karla Medium, above the control, left aligned.

```jsx
<Field label="Getting-ready location" htmlFor="loc" hint="Suite, house, or venue — an address is fine.">
  <Input id="loc" />
</Field>
```

- Required fields take a small Brass middot, never a red asterisk. There is no red in this palette.
- Hints are where the awkward thing goes: travel limits, what won't work on fine hair, why there's no price list.
- Stack fields in a single column. Two-up only for genuinely paired values (date and party size).
