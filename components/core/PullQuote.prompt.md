The pull quote — Instrument Serif italic, Tortoise, on a Tulle field with a 2px Brass keyline on the left. Use it for the promise line, a mission statement, or a closing thought at the foot of a deck slide.

```jsx
<PullQuote attribution="The promise line">We're there before anyone else is.</PullQuote>
<PullQuote tone="plain" size="17px">Early is the one that matters to you.</PullQuote>
```

- The italic is doing the emotional work the upright headline can't. Keep it italic.
- Max ~32 characters per line — this is display type, not a paragraph.
- One per section. Two pull quotes on a page cancel each other out.
