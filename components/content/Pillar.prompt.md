The three message pillars — Early, Yours, Holds — always shown as a set of three, always in that order.

```jsx
<div style={{display:"grid",gridTemplateColumns:"repeat(3,1fr)",gap:2,background:"var(--line-hairline)"}}>
  <Pillar word="Early" fear="Will she actually show up?">Arrival ahead of call time, every wedding.</Pillar>
  <Pillar word="Yours" fear="Will I look like myself?">The reference photo is a starting point, not a target.</Pillar>
  <Pillar word="Holds" fear="Will it fall out by dinner?">Pinned properly, with a touch-up kit left behind.</Pillar>
</div>
```

- The order is the hierarchy: **Early** leads partner-facing material, **Yours** leads bride-facing material, **Holds** does its work in captions and FAQs.
- Never four pillars. Never reorder them.
- `tone="inverse"` uses the raised Ink panel — that is how the partner deck sets them.
