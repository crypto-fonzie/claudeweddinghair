A review, in the bride's own words. Shown three-up on the deck and the site.

```jsx
<Testimonial credit="Bride, Kortright Centre, 2025">
  She was set up before I'd finished my coffee, and nothing that morning ran late.
</Testimonial>
```

- Choose reviews that praise **the morning**, not the hairstyle. The words to look for are calm, early, on time, relaxed, felt like myself.
- Keep the quote to two sentences. Long testimonials read as unedited.
- Curly quotation marks, no bold, no highlighting of key phrases.
