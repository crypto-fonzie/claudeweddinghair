Opens every section and every deck slide: a Tortoise eyebrow, then an Instrument Serif headline, then an optional Karla lede.

```jsx
<SectionHead eyebrow="How a morning runs" headline="You get the schedule before we do." />
```

- Headlines are statements, not labels. "Hair only. On location. Across the GTA." — plain words, full stops.
- Keep the headline under ~18 characters per line so it breaks into two or three short lines.
- `align="center"` only on a cover. Body copy and ledes are always left aligned.
